NWD K2501 · 1024×600 · mdpi. The device is clickable. Use this panel to fake car signals and try your open choices.

Design notes

  • Silent start: when the car starts or a phone reconnects, the stereo or phone may start playing on its own. With "Resume music" off (the default), the launcher sends one pause command and nothing plays until someone presses PLAY. It never starts playback itself.
  • The black display band is the hero: song or station in 32 px white on black, plus a big readout. Everything else is sized for a glance.
  • Daylight contrast: black text on a silver body, white labels on black pads, and no faded or engraved text in day mode.
  • PHONE and SWITCH both open the stock BT phone app. SWITCH should open its device list directly; I'll check whether that app has a direct entry point for it on the unit.
  • The volume knob shows the level set by the steering wheel or rotary knob. Tapping it opens Sound / EQ. The launcher never changes the volume itself.
  • The tape counter shows time since the last track change. Bluetooth (AVRCP) doesn't send the song's position.
  • Helvetica Neue is a paid font. The Android app needs a licence to bundle it, or a free lookalike such as TeX Gyre Heros.
